# Meme Me — Face Meme Matcher 🎭😆 

Meme me is fun, exciting application captures your expressions into memes! Don't just send or consume memes! Interact and make them your own!


Meme Me lives in your browser!

• Meme Me uses AI to detect facial landmarks, analyzing your live webcam expressions and poses in real time, and then finds the closest iconic internet meme to match it.
